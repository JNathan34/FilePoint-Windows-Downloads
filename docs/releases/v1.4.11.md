## What’s new

- **Redesigned tray menu.** The menu shows your connection status and drive letter beside the FilePoint logo. It also has larger action rows, outlined icons, and a separate Quit button.
- **Manage deleted items from FilePoint.** Open **Recycle Bin** from the tray to view deleted items, restore them to their original locations, or permanently delete them if your account allows it.
- **Find the Recycle Bin in Explorer.** The shortcut at the root of the FilePoint drive opens the web Recycle Bin.
