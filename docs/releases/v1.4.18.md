## What’s new

- **Better progress for large Explorer transfers.** FilePoint uploads file data while Explorer copies or moves it, reducing the wait at the end of a transfer.
- **Fewer “File In Use” errors after a transfer.** FilePoint finishes saving earlier so Explorer can rename or move the destination as soon as the copy closes.
