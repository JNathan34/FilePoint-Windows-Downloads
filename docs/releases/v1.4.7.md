**Connected devices** now appears inside the FilePoint drive in Explorer on servers with connected storage enabled. Open it to browse USB drives and phones that your server has mounted and your account can access. Devices use the same icons as the web app. Refresh the folder after connecting or disconnecting a device.

Open **Updates** from the FilePoint tray menu to upgrade. For a fresh install, choose **FilePoint-Setup-arm64.exe** for Snapdragon and other ARM64 PCs or **FilePoint-Setup-x64.exe** for Intel and AMD PCs.
